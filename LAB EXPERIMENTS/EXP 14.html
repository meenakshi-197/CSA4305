About Our System

The online beauty parlour appointment booking system allows customers to search for beauty parlours, browse available services, view stylist profiles, check appointment availability and book appointments online.


Customers can select a service, choose a stylist, select a preferred date and time, provide personal details and make secure payments.


Users can also manage, reschedule or cancel their appointments through their personal dashboard.
