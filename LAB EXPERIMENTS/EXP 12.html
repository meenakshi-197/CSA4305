About Our System

The airline reservation system is a web-based system that consolidates flight schedules, seat availability, flight fares and reservations from different airlines.


Customers and travel agents can search for flights, check real-time seat availability, select seats, enter passenger details and book tickets online.


Users can also manage their reservations, view booking history and receive electronic tickets and confirmation information.
